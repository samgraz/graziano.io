About email from this domain

Email sent from graziano.io includes personal messages and automated system mail, such as:

  • Backup success and failure reports
  • Server monitoring and uptime alerts
  • Software update and security notices

Messages go only to me and a few family members and friends who have asked to receive them. Volume is low, usually a handful of emails per day. This domain never sends marketing or newsletters. Bounces and complaints are monitored, and any address that bounces or reports a complaint is removed right away.
